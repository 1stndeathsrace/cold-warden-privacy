Cold Warden Terms of Service

Effective 18 October 2026

Cold Warden is a Discord bot run by the staff of Cold War, a Roblox game. It works only in the official Cold War community Discord server, where it handles game bans, player reports and ban appeals. By filing a report or appeal, or by using any of the bot's commands, you agree to these terms.

1. Other rules that apply

Using the bot does not replace any other rules. You must also follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, the Roblox Terms of Use, and the rules of the Cold War game and Discord server.

2. Who can use what

  • Anyone in the server can file player reports and appeal a ban on their own Roblox account.
  • Ban, unban and lookup commands are for Cold War staff only.

3. Reports

  • Only report real rule breaking, and give honest information and evidence.
  • False, spam, joke or revenge reports are not allowed.

4. Appeals

  • Only appeal bans on a Roblox account that you own.
  • Tell the truth in your appeal. Do not submit edited or fake evidence.
  • Some bans cannot be appealed. The bot tells you when that is the case.
  • Filing an appeal does not guarantee that a ban is lifted. Staff decide each appeal, and their decision is final.

5. Acceptable use

  • Do not harass, threaten or insult staff or other players in report or appeal channels.
  • Do not spam the bot, try to break or exploit it, or try to get access to staff-only features.
  • Do not post illegal, sexual or otherwise harmful content in channels the bot creates.

Breaking these terms can lead to losing access to the bot's features, a punishment in the Discord server, or action in the game.

6. Availability

The bot is provided free and as it is. It may be offline, change or stop working at any time, with or without notice. As far as the law allows, the Cold War staff are not responsible for any loss caused by using the bot or by it being unavailable.

7. Privacy

What the bot collects and how it is used is explained in the Privacy Policy.

8. Changes

If these terms change, the date at the top is updated. Significant changes are announced in the Cold War Discord server. Using the bot after a change means you accept the new terms.

9. Contact

For questions about these terms, contact a staff member in the official Cold War Discord server.
